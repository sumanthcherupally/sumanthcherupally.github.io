Hi, I'm Sumanth 👋

I build AI systems that hold up in the real world. As a founding AI engineer at Inscope, I take LLM-powered features from research to production: designing the agents, evaluations and safeguards that let AI work through financial data accurately and reliably. Before that, I built large-scale data systems at Barclays and GPU software for self-driving trucks at TuSimple.

I have a master's from UC San Diego, where I studied robotics and AI, and a bachelor's in electrical engineering from IIT Hyderabad.

Outside of work, you'll usually find me on a hiking trail, on the ski slopes, over a chessboard, in the kitchen trying out a new recipe, at the ice rink, or behind a camera somewhere new. A few of those places are on the map below.
